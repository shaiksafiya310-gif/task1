4.Team collaboration:
Description:
------------
Team collaboration is the process where team members work together, 
share ideas, communicate regularly, and coordinate tasks to achieve a
common goal
Flow Representation:
--------------------
Task Assignment
       ↓
Planning & Discussion
       ↓
Work Distribution
       ↓
Development / Execution
       ↓
Communication (Meetings / Updates)
       ↓
Review & Feedback
       ↓
Integration of Work
       ↓
Final Output / Delivery
