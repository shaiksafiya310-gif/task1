1.Agile:
Description:
-----------
Agile is a software development approach where work is done in small,
 manageable parts instead of completing everything at once. It focuses
on continuous improvement, collaboration, flexibility and quick delivery.
Flow Representation:
--------------------
Requirement Gathering
        ↓
Product Backlog (List of all tasks)
        ↓
Sprint Planning
        ↓
Sprint (Development Phase)
        ↓
Daily Stand-up Meetings
        ↓
Testing
        ↓
Review (Demo to client)
        ↓
Feedback
        ↓
Next Sprint (Cycle repeats)
