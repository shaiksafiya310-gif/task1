Scrum:
Description:
Scrum is an Agile framework used to manage and complete complex projects
through small, time-boxed iterations called Sprints. It helps teams work
 collaboratively, deliver faster, and continuously improve.
 Flow Representation:
 Product Backlog
        ↓
Sprint Planning
        ↓
Sprint Backlog
        ↓
Sprint (Development Work)
        ↓
Daily Scrum (Meetings)
        ↓
Increment (Working Product)
        ↓
Sprint Review
        ↓
Sprint Retrospective
        ↓
Next Sprint (Repeat 🔁)
