2.Traditional and agile:
Description:
| Aspect                   | Agile Method                    | Traditional Method (Waterfall)   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Approach**             | Iterative (step-by-step cycles) | Linear (one phase after another) |
| **Flexibility**          | High – changes allowed anytime  | Low – changes are difficult      |
| **Delivery**             | Frequent small releases         | One final delivery               |
| **Customer Involvement** | Continuous feedback             | Only at beginning & end          |
| **Testing**              | Done continuously               | Done after development           |
| **Risk**                 | Low (issues found early)        | High (issues found late)         |
| **Planning**             | Adaptive                        | Fixed planning at start          |
| **Time to Market**       | Faster                          | Slower                           |
Flow Representation for agile:
------------------------------
Requirements
     ↓
Product Backlog
     ↓
Sprint Planning
     ↓
Development (Sprint)
     ↓
Testing
     ↓
Review & Feedback
     ↓
Next Sprint (Repeat 🔁)
Flow Representationfor traditional:
----------------------------------
Requirements
     ↓

     ↓
Implementation (Development)
     ↓
Testing
     ↓

     ↓
